Our Story

Born from loss.
Made with love.

Rion is not just a brand. Rion is a son's name — and every piece of jewellery we create begins not with gold or platinum, but with someone you love, someone you carry in your heart.

Chapter One · 2011

A father. A son.
Three days.

In 2011, our founder lost his infant son. He held him in his arms for three days. Then he said goodbye.

In the silence that followed, a question emerged — one that so many grieving families know by heart: how do you keep the people you love close when they can no longer walk beside you? He searched for an answer that was not a memorial, not a photograph, not a keepsake on a shelf. He wanted something you could wear. Something you could touch. Something that said, quietly and permanently: you are still here.

He discovered memorial jewellery. And in discovering it, he discovered his life's work.

"

A jewel is more than gold. It is a place where love can rest — and travel with you, always.

The Founder
Rion · Est. 2018, Japan

Chapter Two · 2018

Seven years later, Rion
was born.

For seven years, he studied. He visited master artisans across Japan, immersed himself in the centuries-old tradition of Japanese goldsmithing, and developed — through countless trials, setbacks, and refinements — a proprietary technique for completely sealing hair inside fine jewellery. There was no template to follow. He built it from grief, from purpose, and from love.

In 2018, he founded Rion — named after his son. The name is both a tribute and a promise: every piece Rion creates carries someone's story forward. It might be a parent, a partner, a child, a beloved dog or cat. The jewel is the vessel. Love is what fills it.

Every piece is bespoke. Every piece is made entirely in Japan. Every piece holds a story that belongs to you — and only you.

Our Atelier · Japan

Where every jewel is made.

Our atelier is in Japan. Every single piece — without exception — is crafted here, by hand, by skilled artisans who understand what they are making and why. There are no factories involved at any stage. There are no shortcuts. Only craftsmanship, time, and profound care.

We work in K18 gold (yellow, white, and rose), Pt900 platinum, and high-grade diamonds, rubies, and sapphires — all sourced within Japan. The sealing technique was developed and perfected over years of iteration: each piece is completely sealed, designed to last for generations of daily wear.

When your jewel leaves our atelier and travels to your door in Switzerland, it carries with it the full weight of Japanese craft tradition — and the full weight of your love for someone who matters deeply.
