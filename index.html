Our Story

Born from loss.
Made from love.

In 2011, our founder held his infant son for three days — and then said goodbye. In that grief, a question was born: how do we carry the people we love when they can no longer walk beside us?

He discovered memorial jewellery — a ring that could hold a piece of his son close to his heart forever. Seven years later, in 2018, he founded Rion — named after that son. Every piece we create begins not with gold, but with a story. Yours.

"Eternal love. A bond that continues — forever."

A strand of hair from someone dear — a child, a parent, a partner, a beloved pet — becomes a jewel you carry every day. Just 1cm. One strand. That is all we need to keep them with you, always. When words aren't enough, gold endures.

Your questions, answered

Just 1cm in length, one strand. Even less is perfectly fine. For pet fur, the same — 1cm, one strand. We work with whatever you can provide, no matter how little. Many clients worry they don't have enough — you almost certainly do.

A US ring gauge (sizes 1–12, including half sizes) is included in your hair collection kit. Simply try each ring on and tell us which fits. If you already know your US ring size — for example "US 6½" — just let us know and you can skip the gauge entirely.

We ship to the United States — completely free, fully insured, with no import duties. We mail a sterile hair collection kit to your US address. Inside: a sealed envelope, clear instructions, and a prepaid return label. Simply drop the hair in, seal the envelope, and mail it back — we handle all customs. Your finished jewel ships via DHL Express. No surprise charges, no duties — nothing extra on delivery.

Yes. We photograph the hair before sealing, during the sealing process, and after — showing it fully enclosed and hermetically sealed. We send these photos to you via WhatsApp or email before shipping. Complete peace of mind, every step of the way.

Approximately 5.5 months in total — from when we receive your hair in Japan to when your jewel arrives at your door in the US. We send progress updates via WhatsApp or email throughout the entire process. Good things take time — and this jewel is made to last a lifetime.

None whatsoever. We ship to the United States on DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) terms — all import duties and taxes are covered by Rion and included in the price you pay. When your jewel arrives at your door, you owe nothing. The price we quote is the price you pay, period.

Yes, on select designs. Multiple people's hair can be sealed together in one jewel — including hair from family members and pets. This is one of the most meaningful options we offer. Ask us which designs support this when you reach out.

Absolutely. Many of our clients commission pieces for beloved pets — dogs, cats, horses. The kit and process are identical, and instructions for pet fur are included. 1cm of fur is all we need. Every pet deserves to be remembered.

Because each piece is made entirely to your personal order — incorporating your hair, your chosen design, and your written approval — we do not accept cancellations, returns, or refunds once production has begun. Before any work starts, we obtain your written approval of the design and materials. In the event of a manufacturing defect on our part, please notify us within 3 days of receipt — we will remake the piece at no charge.

Any remaining hair — no matter how little — is returned to you in a dedicated container, together with your completed jewel. Nothing precious is ever discarded. It arrives back in your hands along with your jewel.

Your piece is solid K18 gold or Pt900 platinum — exceptionally durable for daily wear. Wipe gently with a soft cloth after wearing. Avoid chlorine, perfume direct contact, and hard impacts. We also offer complimentary lifetime polishing service in Japan — contact us to arrange.
