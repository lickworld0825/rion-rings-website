Our Story
قصتنا
ストーリー

Born from loss.
Made from love.

In 2011, our founder held her newborn son for three days — and then said goodbye. In that grief, a question was born: how do we carry the people we love when they can no longer walk beside us?

She discovered memorial jewellery — a ring that could hold a piece of her son close to her heart forever. Seven years later, in 2018, she founded Rion — named after that son. Every piece we create begins not with gold, but with a story. Yours.

"Eternal love.
A bond that continues forever."

A strand of hair from someone dear — a child, a parent, a partner, a beloved pet — becomes a jewel you wear forever. Just 1cm. One strand. That is all we need to carry them with you, always.

Your questions, answered

How much hair do you need?

Just 1cm in length, one strand. Even less is perfectly fine. For pet fur, the same — 1cm, one strand. We work with whatever you can provide, no matter how little.

How does the collection work from Dubai?

We send a secure kit to your Dubai address via DHL (3–5 days). Inside: a sealed envelope, English & Arabic instructions, and a prepaid return label. Drop it at any DHL point — we handle all customs paperwork.

Can I see the hair being sealed?

Yes. We photograph the hair before sealing, during sealing, and after — showing it fully enclosed and hermetically sealed. We send these photos to you via WhatsApp or email before shipping. Complete peace of mind.

How long does it take?

Total: approximately 5.5 months. approximately 5.5 months total. We send progress updates throughout.

Can I put hair from multiple loved ones in one piece?

Yes, on select designs. Multiple people's hair — or hair and ashes — can be sealed together in one jewel. Ask us which designs support this when you enquire.

Are there any extra charges on delivery?

None at all. All prices are DDP — UAE import duty (5%) and VAT (5%) are prepaid by us and included in the displayed price. You pay nothing extra when your jewel arrives at your door.

Can I order for a pet?

Yes. Many clients commission pieces for beloved pets. The kit and process are identical — the instructions for pet fur are included. 1cm of fur is all we need.

What is your return policy?

Because each piece is made entirely to your personal order — incorporating your hair, your chosen design, and your approved specifications — we do not accept cancellations or returns once production has begun. Before any work starts, we obtain your written approval of the design and materials. In case of a manufacturing defect on our part, we remake the piece at no charge.

What happens to leftover hair after crafting?

Any remaining hair — no matter how little — is returned to you in a dedicated container, together with your completed jewel. Nothing precious is ever discarded.
