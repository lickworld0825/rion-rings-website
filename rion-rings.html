Your questions, answered

How does the hair collection work from Dubai?

We send a secure collection kit to your Dubai address via DHL (3–5 days). It includes a sealed envelope, Arabic & English instructions, and a prepaid return label. You simply collect the hair, seal it, and drop it at any DHL point — we handle all customs paperwork.

Is sending hair from UAE to Japan legal?

Yes, completely. Cut hair (human or pet) is not a restricted item in either UAE or Japanese customs regulations. It is not a biological specimen or hazardous material. All customs declarations on the return envelope are pre-completed by us.

How long does the full process take?

From order confirmation to delivery: approximately 6–10 weeks. This includes kit shipping (1 week), hair return (1 week), and handcrafting (4–8 weeks). We provide updates throughout.

Are there any extra charges on delivery?

None. All prices are DDP (Delivered Duty Paid), meaning UAE import duty (5%) and VAT (5%) are prepaid by us and included in the displayed price. You pay nothing extra when your jewel arrives.

What is your return policy?

In accordance with UAE Consumer Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 15/2020), we offer a 7-day return window. As each piece is uniquely made for you, we work closely throughout production to ensure complete satisfaction.

Can I order for a pet's hair?

Yes. Many clients commission pieces for beloved pets. The process is identical. The collection kit works for pet fur too — instructions are included.
