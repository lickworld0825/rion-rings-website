How it works.

The complete shipping journey from order confirmation to your door — every step handled by Rion.

i

Hair collection kit ships to your UK address

After your order is confirmed and invoice paid, we dispatch a Rion Hair Collection Kit directly to your UK address via DHL Express — free of charge. The kit includes a sterile sealed envelope, a ring size gauge (standard sizes 1–12, half sizes available), clear step-by-step instructions in English, and a prepaid DHL Express return label addressed to our atelier in Japan.

Japan → Your US Address · DHL Express · Free
ii

You return the hair to us

Place a few strands of hair in the sealed envelope, then drop the package at any DHL drop-off location or schedule a free DHL pickup — using our prepaid label. All customs declarations for international shipping are pre-completed by us. There is nothing for you to write, arrange, or pay.

Your US Address → Japan · DHL Express · Free · Prepaid label
iii

Crafting in our Hiroshima atelier

Your hair arrives at our atelier in Hiroshima, Japan. Handcrafting begins. We photograph the hair sealing process and send you a photo report via WhatsApp or email — before, during, and after the seal is closed. You witness every step of your jewel's creation.

Hiroshima, Japan · Approx. 5.5 months
iv

Your finished jewel arrives at your door

After passing our final quality inspection, your jewel is shipped via DHL Express in a Rion presentation box with certificate of authenticity. Fully insured for its full value. All UK import duties and customs fees are prepaid by Rion. Transit time is typically 3–5 business days from Japan. You will receive a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

Japan → USA · DHL Express · DDP · Fully Insured · 3–5 Business Days

Sending hair from the UK

We make it simple. Here's everything you need to know about the hair return process.

Is it legal?

Yes, completely. Human hair is not a controlled substance under US or Japanese law. It is shipped internationally every day via standard couriers. DHL carries hair shipments routinely. Our customs declarations are pre-completed and accurate — there are no legal complications whatsoever.

How much hair?

Very little — 1 to 5 strands is sufficient. No cutting required. Naturally shed hair from a comb, brush, or a haircut you've saved works perfectly. Even a single strand of 1 cm is enough. Full instructions are included in your kit.

How do I send it?

Place the hair in the sterile envelope we send, seal it, attach the prepaid DHL Express label, and drop it at any DHL ServicePoint or drop box — or schedule a free pickup at dhl.com. Everything is pre-labeled and pre-declared. It takes about 5 minutes.

What if something goes wrong?

If your return shipment is delayed or held for any reason, contact us immediately on WhatsApp. We will handle all correspondence with DHL and customs on your behalf. We've never had a hair shipment lost — but if the extraordinary happened, we would work with you personally to find a solution.

Returns & Defects.

Because every Rion piece is bespoke, please read these terms carefully before ordering.

Bespoke Order Policy

Each Rion jewel is made entirely to your personal order — incorporating your hair, your chosen design, your approved specifications, and your engraving.

Before production begins, we obtain your written approval of all details. Once you approve and production begins, the order is final.

  • No cancellations after production begins
  • No returns on completed bespoke pieces (custom goods cannot be resold)
  • Design and material cannot be changed once confirmed
  • Engraving and birthstone can be changed up to the start of production
  • Invoice payment confirms your order

Manufacturing Defects

If you discover a manufacturing defect on our part, we will remake the piece at no charge — including all shipping and customs for the remake. Refunds are not offered.

Contact us via WhatsApp or email within 3 days of receiving your jewel, with clear photographs of the issue. We respond personally and will resolve the situation promptly.

  • Free remake for manufacturing defects
  • All shipping & US customs for remake covered by Rion
  • Contact within 3 days of receipt
  • Resizing available at cost of round-trip shipping (where design permits)
  • Lifetime polishing complimentary — customer pays postage to Japan

Questions about shipping?

Whether you're in New York or Honolulu, we'll walk you through every step. Message us on WhatsApp — we respond personally in English, typically within a few hours.
