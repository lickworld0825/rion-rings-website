The Sealing Process

How your hair
becomes a jewel.

Every Rion piece begins with something irreplaceable — a strand of hair that holds your love. What happens next is a process of precision, reverence, and permanent beauty, carried out entirely by hand at our atelier in Japan.

Not a Locket

The hair is sealed inside the jewel.

Rion is not a locket. The hair isn't placed behind glass or stored in a compartment that can be opened, lost, or exposed.

At our atelier, a skilled artisan seals each strand directly inside the jewel itself, then closes the opening with a cap made of the exact same precious metal — solid K18 gold or Pt900 platinum — for a completely sealed, permanent finish. The hair becomes part of the jewelry itself. It can't be seen from the outside. It simply lives there — forever — close to the person wearing it.

Your questions, answered.

We know entrusting precious hair across the world takes a leap of faith. Here's what we want you to know before you take it.

Is it safe?

Absolutely. Hair is stable, hygienic, and lasting.

Hair is composed of keratin — a stable protein that carries no bodily fluid, poses no health concern, and does not degrade when completely sealed. Egyptian mummies still hold their hair after 4,000 years. Your hair, sealed inside solid gold or platinum, is protected for a lifetime and beyond.

Will the hair be OK during shipping?

Yes. We send you a sealed collection kit designed for exactly this.

When you order, we mail you a Rion hair collection kit — a dedicated protective envelope designed to keep the hair safe during international shipping to Japan. Thousands of customers have used it. Not a single strand has ever been lost.

What if I don't have much hair?

We work with less than you think.

Even a single strand under 1 cm is enough for most of our designs. We understand what you hold is precious and finite — and we treat every last strand accordingly. If you're uncertain about what you have, message us first and we'll guide you.

What happens to the extra hair?

Every strand is returned to you with the finished jewel.

If any hair remains after sealing — which is common when 1–3 strands are sent — it's carefully collected and returned to you inside a dedicated container, placed alongside your finished piece. Nothing is ever discarded. Ever.

The five steps.

From the moment your hair collection kit arrives in Japan to the moment your finished jewel is ready to ship to the United States.

01

The hair arrives

Your sealed collection kit arrives at our Japanese atelier via international post. Inside is the hair of your loved one or pet, carefully enclosed in the dedicated envelope we provided. Even a single strand under 1 cm is sufficient. From the moment it arrives, we handle it with the same care we give the jewel itself.

02

Jewelry production

The K18 gold or Pt900 platinum piece is crafted entirely by hand by our skilled Japanese artisans. Every curve, proportion, and surface is shaped to your approved design — whether a ring, pendant, or fully bespoke creation. The piece must be complete and perfect before the hair is sealed inside.

03

Sealing the hair inside

A small opening is precision-drilled into the jewel. Your hair is placed inside by hand — one strand at a time — with all the care and focus this moment deserves. The opening is then sealed with a cap of the exact same metal: K18 gold or Pt900 platinum. The chamber is then completely sealed: permanent, and designed to last for generations.

Once sealed, the chamber cannot be opened without destroying the piece. This is intentional. The seal is a promise — to you, and to the person whose hair lives inside.

04

Photo report

Before the final polish, we photograph the sealing at every key stage — before the hair enters the jewel, during the sealing, and after the chamber is closed. This complete photographic record is sent to you via email. These images are yours to keep, forever. Many of our customers tell us they treasure the sealing photographs as much as the jewel itself.

05

Final finishing & shipment

The jewel is polished to Rion's exacting standard, passes a final quality inspection, and is placed in the Rion exclusive presentation case and packaging. Only then is it prepared for free insured DHL Express shipment to your US address. The entire process — from the moment your hair arrives in Japan — takes approximately five and a half months.

How much hair
is needed?

Much less than you might expect. We work with the smallest amounts — because we understand that what you hold is precious, irreplaceable, and finite.

✦

Human hair

We recommend 1–3 strands, which gives our artisan the best working range. However, even a single strand under 1 cm is sufficient for most designs. If you're not sure what you have, contact us first — we'll guide you.

◯

Pet fur

Same principle — approximately 1–3 strands recommended, though we can work with less. Fine, short, or delicate fur from any animal is accepted. We treat it with the same reverence as human hair.

◇

Multiple people

Select designs can hold hair from two or more people — or a person and a pet together. Let us know when you reach out and we'll guide you to the right design for your story.

How to collect & prepare your hair.

Simple steps to ensure your hair arrives safely and is ready for our artisans.

1

Wait for your collection kit

After placing your order, we mail you a Rion hair collection kit — a purpose-made dedicated envelope and instructions. Please wait for this kit rather than using your own envelope, so the hair stays protected during international shipping.

2

Collect 1–3 strands

Use clean, dry hands or tweezers. Gently gather 1–3 strands — a small bundle about the thickness of a few threads. If you're collecting from a brush or a keepsake, that's perfectly fine. The hair does not need to be a specific length.

3

Keep it dry and unaltered

Do not wash, treat, or alter the hair before sending. Natural hair — as it is — is exactly what we need.

4

Place in the provided envelope and seal

Place the strands inside the Rion collection envelope and seal it securely. Drop it into the pre-addressed return packaging and ship it back to Japan via the method we specify. That's all — we handle everything from here.

Made to Last

Completely sealed.
For generations.

The sealing technique used at Rion has been refined over years of practice and iteration. Once sealed, the hair is completely enclosed within solid precious metal — designed to withstand a lifetime of daily wear without any degradation.

The sealed chamber is unaffected by water, perspiration, light, or the passage of time. You can wear your Rion jewel every single day, and the hair inside will remain exactly as it was on the day it was sealed — as close to you as your heartbeat.

Important: the seal should never be tampered with. Attempting to open the hair chamber will permanently damage the jewel and void all service warranties. If you ever have a concern about your piece, please contact us by email before doing anything else.
