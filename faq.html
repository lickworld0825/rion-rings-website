Hair & Process

Memorial jewellery is a fine jewel that encloses the hair of someone you love — a person or a beloved pet. The hair is completely sealed inside the ring or pendant, invisible from the outside but permanently part of the piece. You carry them with you, every day, wherever you go.

Anyone's hair — a parent, a child, a spouse, a grandparent, a close friend. We also accept pet hair (dogs, cats, and other animals).

Hair from someone who has passed away is accepted, as is hair from someone still living. Many of our clients create pieces while their loved one is still with them — to carry that connection forward, always. There's no wrong time to begin.

Multiple people's hair can be sealed together in one piece — ask us about this option.

Very little — approximately 1 to 5 strands is sufficient. No cutting required. Naturally shed hair from a comb, brush, or saved from a haircut is perfectly fine. Even a single strand of 1 cm or less works. We include clear instructions in your hair collection kit, so there's no guesswork.

A small chamber is created inside the ring or necklace. Your hair is carefully placed inside by hand, then the opening is sealed with a cap of the same material — K18 gold or Pt900 platinum. The jewel is then completely sealed: watertight and permanent. From the outside, it looks like any fine jewel. The hair is invisible — but it's there, always.

We photograph the process before, during, and after sealing, and send these photos to you via WhatsApp or email. This photo report is included with every order, at no extra charge.

No. The hermetic seal is airtight and permanent. Hair is one of the most durable biological materials — it does not decompose in the absence of moisture and oxygen, both of which are excluded by the seal. Rion pieces are designed for a lifetime of daily wear and intended to be passed down through generations.

Yes. We can seal hair from more than one person — or from a person and a pet — inside the same chamber. This is available on all standard series and is a popular choice for families who want to carry multiple loved ones close at once.

Please let us know at the time of enquiry so we can prepare accordingly.

If you're collecting hair from a loved one who has passed, a funeral home, hospice, or family member can often help. Hair that was saved — from a haircut, a brush, or a keepsake — is entirely acceptable. Please reach out to us and we'll advise you personally on the best approach for your situation. We've helped many families navigate this with care.

The Jewel

All Rion jewels are made in K18 Gold (Yellow, White, or Rose) or Platinum Pt900 — the highest quality, sourced and handcrafted entirely in Japan. For Series C (Birthstone Ring) and Series D (Half Eternity Ring), natural diamonds or gemstones are included as specified. Every element — gold, platinum, diamonds, setting — is Japanese.

Yes. Inner engraving is available on all ring designs — English characters only. A name, a date, a meaningful phrase, or any combination you choose. The engraving is done on the inner band and is visible only to you when you remove the ring. Spaces are not counted as characters.

1–10 characters: CHF59 · From the 11th character: +CHF6 each · Please specify at time of order confirmation.

A ring size gauge is included in your hair collection kit — shipped to your Swiss address. Simply try the gauge and confirm your US size when you return your hair. We work in standard sizes 1 through 12, with half sizes available. If you already know your size, just let us know at the time of order.

Resizing is possible in some cases, depending on the design. However, because the sealed hair chamber is built into the band, resizing requires returning the piece to Japan and can take 2–3 months. We strongly recommend confirming your size carefully using the gauge we send before production begins. The cost of resizing covers round-trip shipping — we handle the rest. Please contact us for a quote before sending.

Yes. Rion pieces are designed for a lifetime of daily wear. The hermetic seal is water-resistant — safe for handwashing, light rain, and everyday use. We recommend removing it during swimming, heavy exercise, or contact with harsh chemicals, which is standard advice for all fine jewellery. See our Care Guide for full details.

Every Rion piece comes with complimentary lifetime polishing at our atelier in Japan. Years or decades from now, you can send your jewel back to us and we'll polish and restore it at no charge. You pay only the shipping back to Japan. This service is included with all pieces, forever — no registration required.

Shipping & Payment

Your finished jewel is shipped via DHL Express from Japan — fully insured for its full value, with a tracking number provided as soon as it's dispatched. Delivery typically takes 3–5 business days from Japan to anywhere in Switzerland after production is complete. All customs clearance is handled entirely by Rion.

All prices are quoted in CHF. We accept credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Diners Club, JCB — via secure Stripe invoice link) and international bank transfer (SWIFT/wire). Full payment is required before production begins.

Because each piece is made entirely to your order — with your hair, to your specifications — it cannot be returned or exchanged unless there is a manufacturing defect.

If there is a defect, we will repair or remake the piece at no charge, including shipping. We take quality seriously and subject every piece to rigorous inspection before shipping. Please see our Shipping & Returns page for full details.

All personal information — your name, your address, and the name of your loved one — is handled with complete discretion and never shared with any third party. We understand the deeply personal nature of what we do, and we treat every client's information and story with the utmost respect. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Switzerland Customers

Yes, completely. Human hair is not a controlled substance under Swiss law and is not subject to import or export restrictions. It is shipped internationally every day — used in the beauty industry, medical research, and keepsake products — via standard international couriers and postal services including Swiss Post, DHL, FedEx, and others.

We ship your hair using Swiss Post with a prepaid return label. All customs declarations are pre-completed by us. You simply drop the sealed envelope at any Swiss Post branch or mailbox. There is nothing complicated or legally ambiguous about this process.

Hair is legally shipped internationally as a standard non-hazardous personal effect. No restrictions apply.
