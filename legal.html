Section 3

Privacy Policy

My Art Inc. (Rion) is committed to protecting your personal information. We operate under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (個人情報保護法 / APPI). California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) — see section 6 below.

1. Information We Collect

For each order, we collect:

  • Name, US shipping address, phone number, email address (or WhatsApp number)
  • Hair provided by you for sealing inside the jewel
  • Payment confirmation details — we do not store card numbers (processed by Stripe)
  • Photographs of the hair sealing process (shared with you; retained as production records)
  • Communication records via WhatsApp or email

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Manufacture and deliver your bespoke memorial jewel
  • Send you photo reports of the production process
  • Handle DHL shipping and US customs documentation
  • Issue invoices and process payments
  • Provide aftercare (lifetime polishing service)
  • Respond to your inquiries

3. Third-Party Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • DHL Express — for international shipping and US customs (name, address, phone)
  • Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor) — card data goes directly to Stripe's servers; Rion never stores or accesses your card number
  • Our Japanese artisans — for production only (first name and order details only; no full address shared)

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information for marketing purposes.

4. Hair Handling

Your hair is treated with the utmost care and respect. Any excess hair remaining after sealing is returned to you with the finished piece in a dedicated container. Nothing is ever discarded.

5. Photography & Image Rights

Photographs taken during the sealing process and of the finished piece are copyright My Art Inc. They are shared with you as part of your personal photo report. You may share these photos on personal social media with attribution to Rion (@rion.rings_official). Commercial use requires prior written consent from Rion.

6. California Residents — CCPA Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: You may request details about the personal information we collect, use, and share
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information — this right is already honored
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any CCPA right
California Privacy Contact To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us via WhatsApp (wa.me/819054621246) or at our registered address above. We will respond within 45 days as required by law.

7. International Data Transfer (US → Japan)

Your personal information is stored and processed at our atelier in Japan. By placing an order, you consent to the international transfer of your data from Australia to Japan. Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) provides substantial safeguards equivalent to leading international privacy standards.

8. Data Retention

Order records (including sealing process photographs) are retained for 10 years for warranty and aftercare purposes. You may request earlier deletion in writing, subject to legal retention requirements.

9. Your Rights (All Users)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct or update inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for processing (where applicable)

To exercise these rights, contact us via WhatsApp or email.

10. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS, encrypted communications, and access-limited internal systems. Hair and physical orders are stored in our secured Japanese atelier.

11. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site, and essential cookies for language preferences. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

12. Contact for Privacy Concerns

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us via WhatsApp (wa.me/819054621246) or at our registered address listed in Section 1 above.
