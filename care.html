Care Guide

Made to last
for generations.

Your Rion jewel is solid K18 gold or Pt900 platinum, hermetically sealed in Japan. With a few simple habits, it'll stay as beautiful as the day it arrived — and long beyond that.

The Rion Promise

Complimentary lifetime polishing.
For as long as you own it.

Every Rion piece includes our lifetime polishing service — completely free of charge. Whenever your jewel needs refreshing, simply send it to our Japanese atelier via DHL. We'll restore it to its original luster and return it to you fully insured. You cover the round-trip shipping; we handle everything else.

Daily care.

Four simple habits that keep your piece looking exceptional every day.

01
✦

Wipe after wearing

After each wear, give your jewel a gentle wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth. This removes skin oils, perspiration, and everyday dust — keeping the metal lustrous and the sealed hair chamber perfectly clear.

02
◯

Store with care

When you're not wearing it, place your jewel in the Rion exclusive case. Store it separately from other jewelry to prevent scratches. Keep it away from direct sunlight, heat, and excess humidity.

03
◇

Remove for activities

Take it off before swimming pools, the beach, hot tubs, saunas, and heavy workouts. Apply perfume, lotion, and sunscreen before putting on the jewel — never spray or apply products directly onto it.

04
✧

Clean gently

For light cleaning at home, a soft polishing cloth is all you need. Avoid ultrasonic cleaners, steamers, and harsh chemicals. For a deep clean or polish, send it to us in Japan — it's free and the results are extraordinary.

Do & Don't.

A quick reference for everyday wear.

Do

  • Wipe with a soft cloth after each wear
  • Store in the Rion exclusive case
  • Remove before swimming, hot tubs, or saunas
  • Apply perfume and lotion before putting it on
  • Send to Japan for free lifetime polishing (you cover shipping)
  • Wear it every day — it's built for it

Don't

  • Expose to chlorine (pools, bleach-based cleaners)
  • Spray perfume or hairspray directly on the jewel
  • Use ultrasonic jewelry cleaners or steamers
  • Scrub with abrasive cloths or toothpaste
  • Knock against hard surfaces or store loosely
  • Try to open or tamper with the hair chamber
