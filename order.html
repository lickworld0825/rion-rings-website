How to Order

Six simple
steps to your
forever jewel.

Every Rion piece is made entirely to your order by hand in Japan. We guide you from first question to doorstep delivery — at no extra cost. We ship your hair collection kit directly to your US address, and handle every customs and import duty, though sales tax may still apply depending on your state.

Free DDP to the USA Email Support Photo Updates

The journey — step by step

From choosing your design to receiving your jewel anywhere in the United States, every step is guided by our team. It takes courage to begin. We make the rest easy.

i
Step One

Browse the Collection

Explore our series — from the Classic Ring (Series A) to the Statement Ring (Series J) — and choose the design that speaks to your heart. Each series is available in K18 Yellow Gold, White Gold, Rose Gold, and Platinum Pt900. If nothing quite fits, our fully bespoke option allows any design imaginable.

View the Collection →
ii
Step Two

Reach Out via Email

Send us a message on Instagram DM, or email with the series and material you're drawn to. You're welcome to ask absolutely anything — about the process, the hair, sizing, engraving, timing, or anything else on your mind. Our team replies within 24 hours, in English.

No commitment required — just a conversation
iii
Step Three

Confirm Your Design & Pay

When you're ready, we confirm all the details together: series, material, engraving if desired, birthstone option, and the final all-inclusive USD price. We then issue your invoice — payable by credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Diners Club, JCB — via secure Stripe link) or international bank transfer. Full payment is required before production begins.

Full payment before production · All prices DDP — no US import duties · sales tax may apply
iv
Step Four

Receive Your Hair Kit

We ship a Rion Hair Collection Kit directly to your US address via USPS — free of charge. Inside you'll find a sterile collection envelope, clear step-by-step instructions, a ring size gauge (US sizes 1–12, half sizes available), and a prepaid USPS return label so you can send the hair back to our atelier in Japan without any hassle or cost.

Shipped to your US address · USPS · prepaid return label included · any hair accepted
v
Step Five

Crafted by Hand in Japan

Once your hair arrives at our atelier in Japan, our artisans begin handcrafting your piece. Each jewel takes approximately 5.5 months to complete — every element done by hand, from casting the metal to placing and sealing the hair. During this time, we send you a photo report showing your loved one's hair being placed inside your jewel, step by step.

Approx. 5.5 months · Photo updates via email · 100% Made in Japan
vi
Step Six

Delivered to Your Door

Once your jewel passes our final quality inspection, we ship it to you via DHL Express — fully insured for its full value, with tracking. Delivery covers all 50 US states, Washington D.C., and US territories. All US import duties and customs fees are prepaid by Rion (DDP terms). Nothing extra to pay at the door beyond any sales tax already shown on your invoice. Just open it.

DHL Express · Fully insured · DDP — no import duties or customs fees on arrival

Everything included.

Every Rion order includes the following as standard — at no additional charge.

i

Hair Collection Kit (to your US address)

Sent via USPS to your address anywhere in the United States. Includes sterile envelope, ring size gauge, instructions, and prepaid USPS return label.

ii

Ring Size Gauge

A sizing gauge is included in your hair kit — so you can confirm your exact US ring size before production begins. Half sizes available (sizes 1–12).

iii

Photo Report

Photographs of your hair being placed and sealed inside your jewel — sent via email during production. You witness every step.

iv

Gift Packaging

Your jewel arrives in a Rion presentation box with tissue and a signed certificate of authenticity.

v

DDP Delivery to the USA

DHL Express, fully insured. All US import duties and customs fees prepaid by Rion — no charges on arrival beyond any applicable sales tax. Free to all 50 states, DC, and US territories.

vi

Lifetime Polishing

Complimentary polishing service at our Japanese atelier, for as long as you own your Rion piece. Ship it back to Japan at any time — we'll restore it at no charge.
